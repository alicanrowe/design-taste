# Banned moves

Each of these was rejected outright. Any one present is a gate fail (rubric G4). Search the render for each line
before showing anything.

## Any medium
- A plain, standard object with one feature attached (a ring on an ankle, a hoop behind a heel, a motif glued
  on a blank panel). The feature must be the structure. ("so stupid and gimmicky")
- Legs or a base that read as a separate stock frame under the body (welded tubes under a cast seat and back, a
  four-leg ring base, a bistro column). The body's own ribs, pipes, lames or strands carry on down to the floor,
  with the tube inside them. ("the legs feel separate from the boddies")
- One part at lower fidelity or in another language than the rest (smooth chrome among carving, a default shell
  under rich hardware). ("doesnt fit the rest of the piece", "inconsistent in quality")
- Large flat, unmodelled panels or plates; flat laser-cut sheet standing in for a sculpted motif. ("a lot of flat
  surfaces", "this sucks")
- Anything thin that dangles, flaps, droops, wobbles or floats free; thin single-layer points, tails and flaps.
  ("flimsy or floppy", "not possible in real life", "no floaters")
- Literal clip-art creatures, bug pictograms, symmetric stencils, butterflies. ("far too literal")
- Feathers or leaf blades used as antennae. (5 strikes)
- Nubs, stubs, end-cap balls, beads on stalks. (8 strikes)
- Evenly spaced rows: bead rows, graded dots, ladder ticks, combs of barbs, milgrain, rope rims, knurling.
  Exception, scoped: a collection whose brief is machining ("an ode to precise machining ... knurling elements or
  other like American industrial kind of things", 21 Nov) uses knurling, even rows and straight rods as its
  language. There the ban is on fake machining: texture instead of cut teeth, gears that could not mesh, parts
  with no job. See domains.md, Machined metal.
- Braided or twisted-rope wire as decoration. ("this braid looks ass") Twisted structural strands that are the
  piece's own language (a twisted foot, a twisted jail bar) are fine.
- Crosses, daggers, fleur-de-lis terminals. ("chrome hearts rip off")
- Melting, drooping, Dali-like drips; lumpy junctions. ("too dali esque", "lumpy tumour")
- Wavy bars and frames. ("wavy as hell")
- The stock picture of a technique: a radial paper fan for "pleated", a plain rope coil for "knot", a flat
  fan of folds stuck round a hub on a wall. ("the project sucks", Plissé v1, 23 Nov)
- Hexagon cages or frames with a block hub. ("i hate the hexagon rework")
- Curled-up toes on feet ("elf shoes"); one stock foot shared across variants.
- Pentagram-like even radials (five equal points in a ring).
- Generic hardware that could go on any object (rectangular stock buckles on a sculptural boot, a tidy handle on a
  gothic case). ("this handle is awful")
- Deep, dark stones (almandine garnet, London blue); mixed stone species on one piece; different stones on a pair.
- Variants that differ only by seed or colour. ("a bit samey", "meh samey")
- Classic-benchmarked lookalikes when the brief asked for creative. ("super ugly and unoriginal")
- Costume or cosplay reads; copying a game character's real garment.
- Glossy grey leather that reads as rubber; milky or untextured stones; low-poly facets; clipping of any kind.
- Open-wire outline panels (a leaf, petal or wing drawn as an empty rim with a few thin struts) at sizes where the
  cells read as holes, about 8 mm across and up. ("kinda ugly", "basic and boring") Model them solid and carved
  (rib and engraved veins, like the loved carved feathers) or fill the cells.
- A stone head held by one visible link, or members that stop as stubs against a setting. ("only connection point
  seems a lil weak")
- In film and reels: hard jump cuts between shots when one continuous move would work; small floating islands
  of terrain in a void where an endless ground plane would do; cartoon puff clouds. ("no jumpcuts", "grass going
  forever into the horizon instead of these floating islands", "the clouds look pretty stupid", 21 Nov)
- In reels and stills: a flat procedural texture as the backdrop (painted brick or block lines with no relief, lit
  head-on so it reads as wallpaper). Build real relief and rake the light across it, or drop the wall (black depth,
  velvet, haze). (VIGIL reel, 22 Nov: "the brick wall on Vigil doesn't look like up to par".)
- In reels: long, even-speed holds on each item (4 to 5 s per piece, slow drifts with nothing new happening).
  ("the timing seemed super awkwardly long pauses", 22 Nov) Ramp speed: move fast between subjects, give each a
  short beat (about 1 to 2 s), and keep a whole social reel under about 50 s unless asked.
- Blunt round capsule ends ("nubs"): a stroke that stops at full width with its round cap showing, in air or half
  out of the member it meets. ("this nub in buyao is ugly", "got some nubs to smooth out") Draw it to a point or
  bury the whole end inside the metal it joins.
- Stems, stalks or tendrils laid across a carved leaf or feather face, melting into it. ("zhang qians vine needs
  fixing") A stem meets a leaf only at the leaf's root.
- Points bolted on: a separate needle cone started inside a fat member, so the member necks to a pinched spike.
  ("feitan needs more smoothing") The point is the member's own taper.
- Any metal inside a ring's finger opening (claws, drops, spikes, struts below the band's inner face). ("this piece
  still has geometry issues")
- Fake findings: an ear wire, post or hair-clip spring modelled fused into the casting. Findings are bought and
  soldered to a cast loop or pad. ("how are the hair clips actually being produced")
- In reels: too much going on. Many pieces crowded into one frame (a lineup of six chairs), or a reel that tours five
  unrelated pieces. One subject doing one thing (modules snapping together), or a tight pair at most. Cut pieces
  until the idea still holds. ("It feels like too much going on", "maybe it should just be like the hooks and that one
  lighting element", 23 Nov)
- In reels and stills of lights: a black void where only the glow shows and the lamp's form disappears. ("It looks
  pretty dark", 23 Nov) Shoot lamps in a white room at dusk: cool low ambient, warm pool of the lamp's own light on
  wall and floor.

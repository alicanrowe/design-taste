the critique arrives, then distil it into `principles.md`, `banned-moves.md` or `rubric.md` (see SKILL.md).

The jewelry sheet pins of 18 Nov (rounds g3, d4, g4, about 120 pins) are logged in full in the
sculpted-metal-casting skill's `client-picks.md`; they are counted into the strikes in `principles.md`.
